The keyboard acts as an **Xbox 360 controller**: each of its 24 controls has two keys. The game
sees the same controller whatever drives it. ReCarbon ships two built-in keyboard presets,
**WASD** (what a new player starts with) and **Arrows**; the tables below show both. For the
interactive view, open the **[Garage](https://retronative.github.io/recarbon/garage/)**.

## What the badges mean

- <StatusBadge status="tested" /> pinned by ReCarbon's unit tests (`key_map_test`, `settings_keys_test`, `profiles_test`, `pad_map_test`).
- <StatusBadge status="automated" /> seen in scripted or virtual-controller runs, not on a physical controller.
- <StatusBadge status="hand-verified" /> checked by a person in a hand test.
- <StatusBadge status="source" /> read from ReCarbon's code or option text, not yet hand-tested.
- <StatusBadge status="pending" /> expected from the option text, waiting for its hand test.
- <StatusBadge status="unverified" /> the control is bound, but what it does in the game isn't known yet: it waits for a hand test.
- <StatusBadge status="not-mapped" /> ReCarbon doesn't read the input (back paddles, the Guide button).

The **key → control** bindings below are all tested, in every preset. Of the **game actions** in
a race, these were seen working in hand tests on the WASD keys (2026-10-25): Throttle, Brake,
Steering, Handbrake (A, **Space**), Speedbreaker (X, **Left Shift**) and Nitrous (B,
**Backspace**, on a car that has nitrous). The camera was also changed by hand, but which key
wasn't recorded. Change Camera (RB), Look Back (LB), Gear Up and Gear Down (the right stick's up
and down, with a manual gearbox), Reverse (the Left Trigger at a standstill) and Pause (Start)
were seen with SDL's virtual controller in a scripted Quick Race (automated), not yet by hand.
With an automatic car, the tester saw K and ; (the right stick's left and right) shift down and
up; that isn't reconciled with the virtual-controller result yet. The rest wait for a hand test.
The physical controller test is deferred: nothing on a real pad is verified.

## Presets and profiles

- **WASD** is the original layout, and a new player's. It drives with W A S D; the arrow keys
  work the left stick (Left and Right steer too) and the D-pad at once, by design. Its stored
  name, in `keyboard.toml`, is `"Default"`.
- **Arrows** drives with the arrow keys; W A S D are the D-pad. No key drives two controls, and
  the left stick's up and down have no key.
- Everything else (Space, Backspace, Shift, Control, Q, E, Return, Escape, O K L ;, Z, C) is the
  same in both.

The website shows these **built-in presets only**. **Custom profiles are configured in game:**

1. Open Settings (F1, Fn+F1 or Cmd+,) and go to the **Controls** tab (Q / E).
2. The **Keyboard Profile** row switches profiles; the change takes effect at once.
3. To rebind, pick a control's row (left and right choose its first or second key), press Space
   (or A), then the new key. **Backspace** (or Y) clears the slot; **Delete** (or X) resets the
   control. The built-in presets can't be changed: editing one makes a copy, such as
   "WASD (custom)".
4. Controller buttons are rebound on the **Controller** tab, which has its own **Controller
   Profile** row. Only buttons can be rebound; the triggers, sticks and deadzones are fixed.

Profiles are saved in `~/Library/Application Support/ReCarbon/profiles/` (`keyboard.toml` and
`controller.toml`). The active keyboard profile's keys are also kept in the `[keys]` table of
`settings.toml`, so an older ReCarbon still finds them. Key names are physical positions,
whatever the layout: `"w"`, `"left_shift"`, `"semicolon"`, `"keypad_1"`, or `"none"`.

F1 opens Settings and can't be bound. Neither can Command, fn or the media keys.

## Controller notes

- Buttons are mapped **by position**: South, East, West, North are A, B, X, Y, so a PlayStation
  pad's Cross is A. The Garage's Xbox / PlayStation switch changes only the names shown.
- <StatusBadge status="automated" /> The default mapping and controller rebinding were checked
  with SDL's virtual controller only. <StatusBadge status="pending" /> No physical controller has
  been hand-tested (deferred), and controller rebinding hasn't been tried on hardware.
- Sticks have a radial deadzone (8% by default) and the triggers their own (2%): Settings > Input.
- **View (Back)** is held back for a moment while it might start the View + Menu chord that opens
  Settings.
- **Back paddles and extra buttons** aren't read by ReCarbon. If the pad's own firmware or
  software remaps a paddle to another button, it acts as that button. Whether a given pad's
  paddles reach SDL at all is device-specific and unverified.

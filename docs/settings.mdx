Press <kbd aria-label="Command">⌘</kbd> + <kbd>,</kbd> (Command + Comma) to open ReCarbon's Settings at any time: in the game, in menus,
during a race.

Also: **F1** (**Fn + F1** on a Mac keyboard, where F1 is the brightness key), or **View + Menu**
(Back + Start) together on a controller.

- **Tabs:** Q / E or LB / RB.
- **Rows:** the arrows, the D-pad or the left stick choose a row and change its value.
- **Close:** Escape or B.

Changes apply at once unless marked *restart*. They are saved to `settings.toml` in
`~/Library/Application Support/ReCarbon/`. An environment variable (for example
`RECARBON_AA=0`) wins over the file for that run, and the menu shows it.

## Technical details

The menu uses players' names. This is what they mean.

### Internal Resolution

The game draws its 3D scene at a multiple of the Xbox 360's 1280×720. The menu names each step by
the standard its size matches exactly; `settings.toml` and `RECARBON_RESOLUTION` keep the scale.

| Menu | Drawn at | Scale (`settings.toml`) |
|---|---|---|
| HD (720p) | 1280×720 | `1x` |
| Full HD (1080p) | 1920×1080 | `1.5x` |
| QHD (1440p) | 2560×1440 | `2x` |
| 4K UHD (2160p) | 3840×2160 | `3x` |
| Auto | picked from the screen and the GPU | `auto` |

- **The row says what is drawn when it isn't what the entry names.** 4K UHD draws 3840×2160 only on
  a display with at least that many pixels. On a smaller one, such as a MacBook's own display, it
  draws QHD, and the row reads "4K UHD (QHD on this display)". Your choice is kept, and a 4K display
  draws 4K again.
- **Auto names what it picked:** "Auto (QHD, 1440p)". A size that matches no standard is shown in
  pixels, never rounded to a name.
- The scene's size is not your screen's resolution: the drawn picture is then scaled to the
  window or screen (see Upscaling). Textures and movies stay at their original resolution.
- `RECARBON_RENDER_SCALE` (for developers) fixes the size for one run, and the row shows it in
  pixels. The game's log names the scale: `[renderer] internal resolution 2x2 (2560x1440, ...)`.

### Upscaling and Sharpening

- **Sharp** is bicubic filtering, **Soft** is bilinear. **FSR 1** is AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1
  (EASU), used when the picture is enlarged; when it isn't, bicubic is used.
- **Sharpening** is a separate pass on top of Sharp or FSR 1 (for FSR 1, the share of its RCAS
  sharpening's full strength). 0 turns it off. It doesn't change how the picture is scaled.

### HDR

HDR (Settings > Display, off by default) comes from SpeedBreaker: Carbon v0.1.1. The game's
picture, its front end included, is decoded to linear light and its highlights are expanded
above SDR white; the grade pass that would clip them reads a floating-point copy of the scene
instead. The settings overlay stays at SDR white.

- **Cost:** measured on a MacBook Air M1 at QHD (2x) in a 1280x800 window, Expanded View on, on
  the same stretch of a race: 36.8 fps with HDR off, 28.9 with it on (about 21% lower; GPU busy
  27.3 to 34.7 ms per frame). About 1.8 ms of it is the final composite pass; most of the rest
  is the renderer's floating-point copies.
- **HDR Type** (`hdr_format`): **macOS HDR** (`edr`) is Extended Dynamic Range (EDR): a 16-bit
  floating-point, extended linear sRGB picture whose highlights reach the display's current
  headroom, at most Highlight Brightness. **HDR10** (`hdr10`) sends a 10-bit PQ (SMPTE ST 2084)
  signal in Rec. 2020 with HDR10 static metadata; macOS still decides whether the display is in
  HDR mode. **Auto** uses macOS HDR on a display that reports EDR headroom, otherwise SDR.
- **Highlight Brightness** (`hdr_strength`): the brightest highlight in multiples of SDR white:
  Normal 2x, Bright 3x, Brightest 4x. A frame that is mostly white (a boot flash, a full-screen
  logo) is held near SDR white, and a large bright area such as smoke is limited to a small
  share of extra light (`RECARBON_HDR_LOCAL_BUDGET`, 0.1 by default; 0 turns it off).
- **HDR10 White Level** (`hdr_paper_white`): SDR white in HDR10, in nits (paper white). Auto is
  100 nits on a Mac. The peak is Highlight Brightness times this: 200 nits by default.
- The line under the HDR rows shows what is in use: `Extended Linear (EDR), up to 1.94x
  (headroom 1.94)` for macOS HDR, `HDR10 (PQ), up to 200 nits`, or `Standard range`.

### Other options

- **Anti-Aliasing:** an FXAA-style pass over the finished picture.
- **Smooth Distant Textures:** mipmaps, the game's own smaller copies of its textures, used in the
  distance. Off draws the full-size texture everywhere, which shimmers.
- **Xbox 360 Picture:** the gamma ramp the game sets for the console's TV output (an Xbox 360
  display gamma). Off shows the picture without it.
- **Expanded View** (on by default) fills a screen that isn't 16:9: on a taller screen such as a
  MacBook's 16:10, the 3D view shows more above and below, and the HUD stays at 16:9. Off shows the
  original 16:9 view with bars. It's saved as `aspect_ratio` (`auto` is On, `16:9` is Off), and
  `RECARBON_ASPECT` overrides it.

Press **F1** to open ReCarbon's Settings at any time: in the game, in menus, during a race. On a
Mac keyboard F1 is the brightness key, so press **Fn+F1**, or **Cmd+,**. On a controller, press
**View + Menu** (Back + Start) together.

- **Tabs:** Q / E or LB / RB.
- **Rows:** the arrows, the D-pad or the left stick choose a row and change its value.
- **Close:** Escape or B.

Changes apply at once unless marked *restart*. They are saved to `settings.toml` in
`~/Library/Application Support/ReCarbon/`. An environment variable (for example
`RECARBON_AA=0`) wins over the file for that run, and the menu shows it.
